Apply the **exact visual look-and-feel of the attached "CBOE pitch July 15 2026.pdf"** to the attached
approved wireframe for the **Cushman & Wakefield → Corum A.M. "Westgate I — Paris Proof"** pitch. Treat the
CBOE deck as the definitive C&W house style. This is a **styling pass only**: do not change the slide order,
the content, the copy or the white Team page — only apply the design system.

## 0. Golden rule
**Preserve the wireframe's structure and copy exactly.** Same slides, same order, same text, same tables and
diagrams. You are skinning it, not redesigning it. If a layout must adapt to the house style, keep every
piece of content and every placeholder marker ("€ [ • ]", "[ PHOTO ]", "[ GANTT ]").

## 1. Sample the design system from the CBOE deck (do not invent)
- **Colours:** sample the palette directly from the CBOE PDF and use it verbatim across the whole deck —
  background, primary accent, dark text, light panels. Do not introduce colours that are not in the CBOE deck.
  (Fallback only if the PDF is unavailable: C&W brand — accent red #C3002B, charcoal #1C1C1C, web teal
  #0093B2, warm greys; white backgrounds.)
- **Typography:** match the CBOE deck's type family, weights and scale (large bold slide titles, lighter
  body). Keep it clean, corporate, generous whitespace.
- **Iconography & lines:** match the CBOE deck's simple line-icon and divider style.

## 2. Reproduce these recurring CBOE components (map them onto our slides)
- **Masthead:** every content slide carries the CBOE-style running header top-left: **"Cushman & Wakefield |
  Corum"** with the slide number in the same position/format as the CBOE deck.
- **Section label + descriptor:** each slide has an UPPERCASE section label (e.g. "UNDERSTANDING THE
  PROJECT", "PROJECT JOURNEY", "WHY CUSHMAN & WAKEFIELD") with a single sentence-case descriptor line
  beneath it — exactly the CBOE pattern. Our wireframe kickers/action titles map straight onto this.
- **Statement / pull-quote slides:** render our governing-thought and 2030-vision slides in the CBOE
  "statement" style — large, minimal, brand-accent text with short triads. Echo the CBOE cadence
  ("Design continuity. Delivery accountability.") with **our** message:
  **"One programme. One accountable partner. PwC aligned."** and keep the footer badge
  **"CORUM DECIDES · C&W DELIVERS · PwC ALIGNED"** styled as a small brand chip.
- **Value triple:** style the "success factors / shared approach" content as a CBOE-style three-pillar row
  (like "Excellence · People & Performance · Delivery with Certainty").
- **Project journey:** render our timeline as the CBOE **numbered-step journey** grouped into phases with
  durations and clear decision gates (CBOE used "PHASE 1: DESIGN & ENGINEERING | 16W" → "PHASE 2:
  CONSTRUCTION | 20W", 10 numbered steps). Keep our phase names (Phase 0 → 3) and indicative dates.
- **Team page:** apply the CBOE team-grid aesthetic to the section, **but keep the Team slide itself
  deliberately BLANK/white** — only the section label "Team", nothing else. (Do not populate it.)
- **Tables:** style the decision-rights/RACI table, risk table and fee table like the CBOE "Cost Structure"
  tables — clean rules, brand header row, generous padding. Keep our RAG dots (red/amber/green).
- **Two-scenario comparison (M3E):** style as two elegant comparison panels consistent with the CBOE
  layout language.
- **Stakeholder map & org chart:** render as clean C&W-style node diagrams with C&W as the highlighted hub.
- **Track record:** style like the CBOE "A sneak preview of C&W projects" case pages (image + short
  descriptor), with a "Project & Development Services" style footer label.
- **Contact & cover:** cover in the CBOE cover style (client name | building, date); closing contact block
  with Sjoerd Bezemer and Frank Okker, in the CBOE contact-page style.

## 3. Voice & tagline
Keep the C&W tone: confident, precise, service-led. Let our **"single point of control"** carry the same
energy as CBOE's **"one partner. total control."** Reinforce the twin promise everywhere: **we deliver the
works**, and **PwC stays fully aware and aligned**.

## 4. Do NOT
- Do not reorder, add or remove slides. Do not rewrite the copy. Do not fill the Team page.
- Do not use colours, fonts or effects that are not consistent with the CBOE deck.
- Do not turn placeholders into invented content — keep them visible for later completion.

## 5. Output
A fully styled, presentation-ready PPTX in C&W CBOE house style, same 24-slide structure as the wireframe,
editable (real text boxes and shapes), with all placeholder markers intact and the Team page left white.
