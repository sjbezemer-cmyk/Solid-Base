## THE CBOE LAYOUT SYSTEM (reproduce these — seen in the attached deck)
- **No coloured left spine.** Clean top-left header on every content slide:
  **amber UPPERCASE section label** + a **navy sentence-case descriptor** directly beneath it.
- **Footer** small grey: "Cushman & Wakefield | Corum" + page number.
- **Section sub-headings**: small **amber bold** label + **navy** body, often with a **thin amber vertical
  rule** to the left of the column.
- **Pull-quotes / key questions**: **amber serif, larger**, e.g. CBOE's "How can the 19th floor…?" —
  use this for our SCQA Question and the governing statement.
- **Tiles / cards**: rounded; the highlighted tile = **amber fill + navy text**; others = **navy fill +
  white text** or **cream fill + navy text**.
- **Phase bars** (journey/planning): a full-width bar with icon — **amber fill "PHASE 1 … | 16W"** and
  **navy fill "PHASE 2 … | 20W"**, white text — with **numbered circles** (amber for phase 1, navy for
  phase 2) on a connector line, each circle a navy sub-heading + grey body.
- **Side panels**: full-height **navy panel + white/amber text** (e.g. team/oversight), with **thin amber
  vertical rules** as separators.
- **Photography**: architectural imagery of Westgate I / Amsterdam, often as a right-hand column or a
  full-bleed cover with a navy overlay; amber title, white subtitle, amber date; a short **serif tagline**.
- **Statement slides**: full **navy** background, amber section label, large white statement, a short
  **amber accent rule**, and the golden-thread chip.
- **Golden-thread chip** (every content slide except cover, Team, and the closing contact bar):
  small **navy chip with amber text** "CORUM DECIDES · C&W DELIVERS · PwC ALIGNED".
