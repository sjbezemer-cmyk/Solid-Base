---

Design a 16-slide business development pitch deck (16:9, PowerPoint) for Cushman & Wakefield
Project & Development Services Netherlands, proposing project management services to real
estate investor **Corum A.M.** for the **"Westgate I — Paris Proof"** sustainability upgrade
in Amsterdam. The attached wireframe PPTX defines the exact slide order, content and
annotations — follow it slide by slide; the copy on the wireframe is near-final unless marked
as placeholder.

## Brand & style
- Cushman & Wakefield corporate identity: primary accent **CW_RED #C3002B**, text/dark
  **CW_DARK #1C1C1C**, white backgrounds, generous whitespace.
- Typography: clean modern sans-serif (Helvetica Neue / Arial family), large confident slide
  titles in uppercase, red kickers/section labels above titles.
- Style reference: premium consultancy tender deck — think of C&W's ESA ESTEC proposal
  ("REBUILDING FOR TOMORROW"): a thin red section spine on the left edge, small footer
  "CUSHMAN & WAKEFIELD | CORUM A.M." with page number, numbered contents blocks (01–05).
- Photography: architectural imagery of the Westgate I office building in Amsterdam
  (full-bleed on cover; duotone or subtle dark overlay so white/red type stays readable).
  Use tasteful placeholders where no photo is supplied.
- Language: English. Tone: confident, precise, service-minded — no hyperbole.

## Content anchors (must stay accurate)
- Client: Corum A.M. (contacts: Dirk Hooiveld, Senior Technical Manager; Marcus Dillon).
- Building: Westgate I, Thomas R. Malthusstraat 5, 1066 JR Amsterdam, ±27,000 m²,
  tenant PwC, building in full operation.
- Objective: technically upgrade to **Paris Proof — a hard contractual agreement**.
- Two technical scenarios by advisor M3E (9 Feb 2026): PREFERRED = low-temperature WKO
  (thermal storage) + climate ceilings, full replacement of gas installation, phased
  floor-by-floor; ALTERNATIVE = air-water heat pump + retained VAV induction units + VRF
  zone control + new DX block, fewer interventions but residual VAV life of 6–9 years.
- C&W role: project manager on behalf of the owner; **two-PM model**: a Stakeholder
  Management PM (tenant relations, communication, decision-making) and a Technical PM
  (technical coordination, site management, M3E alignment, tender & execution).
- Phased engagement: Phase 0 preparatory assignment → then three separate assignments:
  PM Preparation, PM Execution, Building Consultancy (quality inspections).
- Core message throughout: tenant satisfaction first, phased delivery in a live building,
  clear governance, active risk management, **no surprises**.

## Slide-specific directions
1. **Cover** — full-bleed Westgate I photo, red accent bar, title "WESTGATE I — PARIS PROOF",
   subtitle "Project Management Proposal", "Prepared for Corum A.M. · July 2026", C&W logo.
2. **Contents** — five numbered blocks (01–05) as in wireframe.
3. **Cover letter** — letter block left, three summary tiles right (Understanding / Approach /
   Commitment), contact strip with Sjoerd Bezemer and Frank Okker.
4. **Understanding the project** — facts column + a clean stakeholder diagram (Corum, PwC,
   M3E, Contractor, with C&W in red at the centre as connector).
5. **Two routes to Paris Proof** — two comparison panels (preferred in red header, alternative
   in dark header), closing banner line about our approach being robust for either scenario.
6. **Assignment & role** — narrative left, six numbered responsibilities right.
7. **Key success factors** — four tiles.
8. **Phased approach** — four phase cards with arrows, Phase 0 highlighted in red.
9. **Process planning** — clean indicative Gantt (Q3 2026 → 2028) with red decision-milestone
   diamonds; add footnote "indicative — to be validated with M3E design planning".
10. **Project organisation** — org chart, the two PM boxes in red as the visual heart.
11. **Governance & communication** — meeting-structure table + governance principles panel +
    reporting bar (one-page monthly report visual).
12. **Risk management** — styled risk table with RAG dots (use red/amber/green).
13. **Team — LEAVE THIS PAGE WHITE/EMPTY.** Only the small section label "04 — TEAM" in the
    margin and page number. No content, no placeholders. It will be filled in later.
14. **Track record** — four reference cards (photo, project, size, role, result) as elegant
    placeholders.
15. **Fee proposal** — fee table per phase (amounts as "€ [ • ]"), reference rate card panel,
    assumptions & exclusions (incl. DNR 2011).
16. **Next steps & contact** — five numbered steps ending in a red contact banner.

## Deliverable
A polished, presentation-ready PPTX (editable, real text boxes — not images of text),
consistent margins and grid, 16 slides. Keep all placeholder markers ("[ • ]", "[FOTO]")
visible so they are easy to find and replace.
